About BMI

What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight that applies to adult men and women.

How is it calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) / (height (m))². It provides a general indicator of whether a person is at a healthy weight.

Important Note

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic measure. Consult healthcare professionals for personalized health advice.
